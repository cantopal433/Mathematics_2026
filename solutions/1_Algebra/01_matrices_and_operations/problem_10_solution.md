## Solution

$$
Ab_1=A\begin{pmatrix}1\\ -1\\ 3\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\ 2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Ab_2=A\begin{pmatrix}2\\ 0\\ 1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\ 1\end{pmatrix},
$$

$$
AB=\begin{pmatrix}-1 & 2\\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Column $j$ of $AB$ uses only column $j$ of $B$, so it equals $Ab_j$.

## Verification

The columns of $AB$ are exactly $Ab_1$ and $Ab_2$.
