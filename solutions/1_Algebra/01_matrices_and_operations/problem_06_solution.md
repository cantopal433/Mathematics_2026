## Solution

The transpose turns rows into columns:

$$
A^T=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4\\ 2 & 5\\ 3 & 6\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B^T=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 & -1\\ 0 & 1 & 3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
AB=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 11\\ 8 & 23\end{pmatrix}.
$$

## Verification

$$
(AB)^T=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 8\\ 11 & 23\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B^TA^T=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 8\\ 11 & 23\end{pmatrix},
$$

so $(AB)^T=B^TA^T$.
