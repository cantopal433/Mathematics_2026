## Question

The vectors

$$
u=
\begin{pmatrix}
3 \\
1 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad v=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

span a parallelogram. Compute its area using a determinant. Then reverse the order of the vectors and explain what changes in the determinant and what does not change in the area.

## Solution

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & 1 \\
1 & 4
\end{pmatrix}=11,\qquad \mathrm{Area}=11.
$$

Reversing the vectors gives $\det=-11$: the sign changes, the area $|\det|=11$ does not.

## Verification

$1\cdot1-3\cdot4=-11$, and $|-11|=11$.
