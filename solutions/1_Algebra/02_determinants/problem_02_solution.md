## Question

For

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & -1 \\
0 & 3 & 4 \\
2 & 1 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

compute $\det A$ using Sarrus' rule. Then perform one simple check using a chosen property of the determinant.

## Solution

Sarrus' rule:

$$
\det A=(15+16+0)-(-6+4+0)=31+2=33.
$$

## Verification

Expansion along the first column: $1\cdot11+2\cdot(8+3)=33$.
