## Question

For

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 3 & 1 \\
2 & 1 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

compute $\det A$. Then form a matrix $B$ by swapping the first and second rows of $A$.

First predict $\det B$ using a determinant property, and then verify your prediction by calculation.

## Solution

$$
\det A=1\cdot(3-1)-2\cdot(0-2)=6.
$$

A row swap changes the sign, so $\det B=-6$.

## Verification

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 3 & 1 \\
1 & 2 & 0 \\
2 & 1 & 1
\end{pmatrix}=0-3\cdot1+1\cdot(1-4)=-6.
$$
