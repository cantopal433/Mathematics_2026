## Question

Without expanding the determinant, compute

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 4 & 1 \\
0 & -3 & 5 \\
0 & 0 & 7 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

State the rule for an arbitrary triangular matrix and explain it using this example.

## Solution

For a triangular matrix, $\det$ is the product of the diagonal entries:

$$
\det=2\cdot(-3)\cdot7=-42.
$$

## Verification

Expansion along the first column: $2\cdot(-21-0)=-42$.
