## Solution

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4
\end{pmatrix}=12+10=22,\qquad
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14
\end{pmatrix}=14-14=0.
$$

Only the first matrix is invertible, since $\det\ne0$.

## Verification

In the second matrix the second row is twice the first, so $\det=0$.
