## Question

For

$$
A(t)=
\begin{pmatrix}
t & 1 \\
2 & t \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

find all values of $t$ for which the matrix is not invertible. Explain how your answer is related to the determinant being zero.

## Solution

$$
\det A(t)=t^2-2=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\pm\sqrt2.
$$

For these $t$ the matrix is not invertible, because $\det=0$.

## Verification

For $t=\sqrt2$: $(2,\sqrt2)=\sqrt2\cdot(\sqrt2,1)$, so the rows are dependent.
